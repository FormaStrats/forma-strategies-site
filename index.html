How it works

Three clicks. Delivered in minutes, not days.

No account opening, no custodian, no settlement wait. Pick a strategy and approve once. Every position lands directly in your wallet.

01

Connect your wallet

Phantom, Solflare, Backpack or any Solana wallet. Forma never asks for keys and never holds funds.

02

Choose a strategy

Pick a committee-approved strategy (index, thematic, tracker, multi-asset or active), then enter an amount above its minimum (from $50; $250 for the Total Index).

03

Approve once

One approval covers the whole portfolio. Jupiter lands each trade in seconds, and the tokens arrive in your wallet.

The separate account, rebuilt

Execution

Best execution, verified on every position.

The Forma engine treats each position like an institutional order: routed for the best price, checked against fair value, and refused if it doesn't measure up.

Meta-aggregated routing

Every position routes through Jupiter, across AMMs and RFQ market makers, for the best available fill.

Fair-value price protection

Routes are checked against the underlying stock price. If the best price misses your threshold, that position waits as USDC instead of overpaying.

One approval

Up to 20 positions are signed together and landed by Jupiter in seconds. A typical 17-position deploy needs one approval.

Receipts for every leg

Each trade is a public Solana transaction. Your receipt links every leg to Solscan, with cost basis and P&L tracked per account.

Security & transparency

Your assets never leave your hands.

Self-custody

No vault, no pooled fund, no withdrawal queue. Positions settle into your wallet and stay there until you move them.

User-signed

You approve every transaction in your own wallet. Your wallet shows each balance change before you sign. Forma never holds keys.

Verifiable

Every deploy, rebalance and withdrawal is a public onchain transaction. Account ledgers stay in your browser and export with one click.
