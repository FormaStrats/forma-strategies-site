    Execution and custody

    Nothing moves without your signature.

    Self-custody

    No vault, no pooled fund, no withdrawal queue. Positions stay in your wallet until you move them.

    Price limits

    Each trade is checked against a fair-value price, the underlying stock where available. If the route is worse than your limit (2.5% by default), that position waits in USDC.

    Batched approvals

    Up to 20 positions are signed together and routed through Jupiter.

    A receipt for every trade

    Each trade is a public Solana transaction. Receipts with cost basis link to every leg.

    Governance

    Every decision on the record.

    Strategies launch only by a vote of Forma's Global Investment Committee (GIC), under a published charter. Risk limits are enforced in code.
