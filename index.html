Forma Strategies provides non-custodial software and general model portfolios. It is not a broker-dealer, custodian or investment adviser, and nothing on this site is investment advice, a recommendation, or an offer to buy or sell any security.

Forma never holds your assets or keys; issuers can freeze or pause their tokens. Tokenized stocks (for example xStocks, issued by Backed Assets (JE) Limited) track but are not the underlying shares. They carry no voting rights, carry issuer, custody and smart-contract risk, and can trade away from the underlying price, especially outside US market hours. They are not available to U.S. persons or in sanctioned or restricted jurisdictions; check yours in Who can hold.

The homepage simulation is illustrative, not a real account: sped up, with the model’s live weights when available and an assumed trading cost (routing fees and spread). A real deploy also needs a little SOL for network fees and refundable token-account deposits: about 0.06 SOL for 21 holdings. Live prices are third-party data and can be delayed or wrong. Digital assets are volatile and you can lose money. Products marked in development are not available yet.
