## Business Overview

AbbVie is a diversified biopharmaceutical company navigating a critical inflection point: the loss of exclusivity (LOE) on Humira (adalimumab), which generated ~$20B in peak annual revenue. The company's survival thesis centers on the successful ramp of three next-generation franchises launched post-Humira decline: Skyrizi (risankizumab, IL-23 inhibitor for psoriasis and inflammatory bowel disease), Rinvoq (upadacitinib, JAK1 inhibitor for rheumatoid arthritis and other immune diseases), and Vraylar (cariprazine, atypical antipsychotic for bipolar disorder and depression). Collectively, these three assets represent the company's path to value; they are now the primary revenue drivers as Humira erodes from biosimilar competition globally.

AbbVie's revenue mix is undergoing dramatic rebalancing. Humira represented ~50% of revenues in 2023 but has declined to estimated 35–40% by 2025 as biosimilars penetrate key markets, especially in Europe and increasingly in the U.S. (where Hadlima and Hufilada launched in late 2024). Skyrizi is the crown jewel: now generating $3–4B annually with high growth runway (20–25% CAGR) as IL-23 becomes the preferred immunology mechanism. Rinvoq is ramping across multiple indications (RA, psoriasis, IBD, lupus) with peak sales potential of $4–5B. Vraylar is now a $2B+ asset with momentum in depression and bipolar disorder. The company maintains a smaller but stable oncology portfolio (Imbruvica in chronic lymphocytic leukemia, facing competition but still $1B+ annually) and contributes meaningful revenue from lower-profile assets. The strategic imperative is crystal clear: convert AbbVie from a Humira-dependent monolith into a broad-based immunology and psychiatry player.

AbbVie's near-term risk is execution: Humira's decline must be fully offset by Skyrizi, Rinvoq, and Vraylar growth by 2027–2028, or the company faces significant EPS headwinds. The company has guided to flat-to-modest revenue growth through 2026 but targets return to mid-single-digit growth by 2027 as new products mature. The longer-term competitive risk is market saturation in IL-23 (competitors include Merck's Skyrizi competitor in development, Lilly's therapies in obesity) and JAK inhibitor commoditization due to generic/biosimilar competition. Pipeline depth in oncology and neuroscience will be critical to sustaining mid-single-digit growth beyond 2027.

## Revenue Architecture

AbbVie's revenue is generated across immunology, oncology, neuroscience, and a legacy portfolio weighted toward maintenance therapies. Immunology (Skyrizi, Rinvoq, residual Humira) represents ~50% of revenues and is the growth engine. Skyrizi's margin profile is excellent—high-priced biologics with limited generic competition—and peak sales could reach $5–6B if penetration extends to acne, atopic dermatitis, and international markets. Rinvoq operates in a competitive JAK space but maintains pricing power due to superior efficacy and tolerability profiles relative to competitors; peak sales potential is $4–5B across multiple indications.

Neuroscience/psychiatry (Vraylar, generics in decline) represents ~20% of revenues. Vraylar is increasingly critical as the company's psychiatric franchise anchor, particularly given secular tailwinds in depression and bipolar disorder treatment prevalence. Oncology (Imbruvica, others) is mature and declining; these assets face competitive pressure from newer agents but retain stable revenues of $1–2B annually. The critical metric is the degree to which Skyrizi and Rinvoq offset Humira's decline. Each 10% drop in Humira sales must be matched by equivalent growth in the new franchises to maintain flat revenue. Capital intensity is moderate—pharma R&D is 15–18% of revenue—but the company is spending aggressively on clinical programs to deepen the pipeline beyond near-term franchises.

## Competitive Moat

AbbVie's moat in immunology is intellectual property and manufacturing scale. Skyrizi and Rinvoq have multiple years of patent protection (through 2030s), and the company controls significant capacity for biologics manufacturing. However, the moat is narrowing: IL-23 pathway blockade is becoming crowded (other players developing similar agents), JAK inhibitors are commoditizing, and the company has limited true first-mover advantages in newer mechanisms. Vraylar has patent protection but faces competition from generic and branded antipsychotics.

The company's real competitive advantage lies in distribution scale and payer relationships built over decades of Humira dominance. The installed base of gastroenterologists, rheumatologists, and dermatologists familiar with AbbVie's immunology products provides some switching friction. However, this is temporary; if Skyrizi and Rinvoq do not deliver superior clinical/economic value, payers and physicians will readily switch to competitors' agents. The pipeline is the long-term moat builder; success in oncology, neuroscience, or next-generation immunology agents could reestablish AbbVie as a growth story, but execution risk is material.

## Key Investment Considerations

**Bull Case:** AbbVie is a classic post-patent-cliff compounder if new franchises execute on plan. Skyrizi reaching $5B+ in peak sales and Rinvoq establishing itself as a JAK standard-of-care would generate sufficient revenue to offset Humira's LOE entirely. Margins could expand as lower-margin legacy products decline and higher-margin newer biologics scale. If the company can reignite the pipeline with meaningful oncology or neuroscience advances, EPS growth could reaccelerate to 8–10% annually by 2028–2030. The stock's valuation multiple reflects ongoing uncertainty; if transition execution de-risks, significant upside is available.

**Bear Case / Primary Risks:** (1) **Skyrizi Saturation** — If IL-23 penetration peaks below expectations or payer resistance to reimbursement intensifies, Skyrizi's peak sales could fall short of $5B, leaving a revenue gap that Rinvoq and other assets cannot fill. (2) **Rinvoq Competition** — JAK inhibitors are becoming generic/biosimilar-eligible; if regulatory pathways for biosimilar JAK inhibitors accelerate, Rinvoq's exclusivity window and pricing power erodes faster than currently modeled. (3) **Humira Decline Acceleration** — If U.S. biosimilar adoption accelerates beyond current assumptions, Humira could decline 30–40% annually, overwhelming any offset from new products. (4) **Pipeline Execution** — Beyond Skyrizi and Rinvoq, the pipeline is thin; a Phase III failure in a late-stage program would reset expectations for post-2027 growth.

**Due Diligence Focus Areas:**
- Humira sales decline trajectory by geography (U.S., Europe, emerging markets) and biosimilar penetration rates; this is the denominator for all other growth metrics
- Skyrizi revenue growth rate YoY and market share within IL-23 space; watch for plateauing growth or payer pushback
- Rinvoq label expansion progress (RA, psoriasis, IBD, lupus, others) and peak sales estimates by indication
- Vraylar growth in depression indication and market share in atypical antipsychotics; secular demand growth should support this asset
- Pipeline depth in oncology and neuroscience with Phase II/III timelines; this determines long-term growth visibility post-2027

## Valuation Archetype: Post-Patent-Cliff Compounder

**Primary Metrics to Track:**
- **Humira Decline Rate** — Monitor annual decline % by geography; model should assume 20–30% decline in U.S., 35–45% in Europe; variance here cascades to overall revenue guidance
- **Skyrizi Revenue & Growth Rate** — Track absolute revenue and YoY growth %; target is 20%+ growth through 2027, then moderation to 10–15% as market matures
- **Rinvoq Ramp Trajectory** — Monitor revenue by indication and cumulative patient numbers; validate that label expansions are translating to volume
- **Vraylar Momentum** — Track revenue and market share in depression; secular tailwinds should drive 10%+ annual growth through 2027
- **Pipeline Advancement** — Monitor Phase II/III readouts and clinical POC for next-generation immunology and oncology programs; this de-risks post-2027 growth
