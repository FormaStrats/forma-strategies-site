Built for the Next Generation of Asset Management

Forma Strategies is a provider of noncustodial digital asset strategies, exclusively built on Solana. Forma Strats was founded on the premise of enhancing user experience for self custodied digital assets across the world.

Our mission is to provide strategic allocation across the Solana ecosystem through model-based strategies that mirror traditional separately managed accounts (SMAs) with enhanced blockchain mechanics and transparency.

Based in Boston, Massachusetts, we're pioneers in financial technology—bringing institutional-grade asset management onchain while maintaining the core principles of decentralization and self-sovereignty.
