## Business Overview

AppLovin is a mobile advertising and app monetization platform operator serving the global app economy (2.6B+ smartphone users globally). The company operates two core business units: (1) **AppLovin Platform** (formerly Software)—AXON AI engine for app developers and publishers to monetize user audiences through programmatic in-app advertising, plus AppDiscovery (internally developed gaming titles used as user-acquisition channels for third-party publishers); (2) **Adjust** (formerly Acquired Intelligence)—post-attribution analytics, fraud detection, and user acquisition optimization for mobile app developers.

The AXON 2.0 AI engine is the core platform thesis: AXON uses reinforcement learning to optimize real-time bidding (RTB) on mobile ad inventory across the AppLovin network. Instead of rule-based bidding, AXON learns advertiser intent and inventory quality patterns, achieving superior return-on-ad-spend (ROAS) relative to competitor platforms (Google's Pmax, Meta's Advantage+). This 2–5% ROAS advantage translates to higher fill rates (% of impressions monetized), higher CPMs (cost per thousand impressions), and stickiness—advertisers achieve better campaign performance and remain locked into AXON.

AppDiscovery is strategic: AppLovin owns a portfolio of internally developed mobile games (Wordscapes, Merge Dragons, Homescapes) with 100M+ monthly active users. These games serve as a self-supply channel for AXON, providing zero-cost-of-capital user-acquisition inventory that AppLovin can programmatically buy and sell within its platform, improving fill rates and AXON learning velocity. Recent strategic context includes AppLovin's attempted acquisition of gaming assets and TikTok (now concluded), reflecting management's conviction that owning end-user audiences and leveraging them through AXON represents structural competitive advantage.

## Revenue Architecture

AppLovin's revenue structure has undergone significant evolution: platform revenue (~$1.5–1.7B annually) is pure SaaS/advertising, with blended gross margins of 65–75% (high-margin programmatic ad inventory monetization). Adjusted EBITDA margins have expanded from 15–20% (2022–2023) to 30–40% (2024–2025), driven by (1) AXON adoption reducing operational complexity, (2) AppDiscovery user-acquisition leverage (owned inventory reduces external CAC), and (3) operating leverage in data centers and infrastructure.

The critical KPI is revenue per user (RPU) growth: AXON 2.0 improvements drive higher CPMs and improved fill rates, yielding 5–10% annual RPU expansion. Combined with 15–20% growth in advertiser spend (macroeconomic resilience in mobile gaming and performance marketing), platform revenue has scaled to $1.5–1.7B at 35–50% YoY growth rates.

Adjust revenue (~$300–400M annually) is slower-growing (5–10% YoY) due to market saturation (post-attribution is a commoditizing category as major platforms integrate attribution in-house). Management has begun rationalizing Adjust, focusing on high-value verticals (gaming, non-gaming apps) and reducing low-margin enterprise segments. Blended Adjust margins are 30–40%, making it a profitable but low-growth division.

Operating expense discipline has improved materially: AppLovin has shifted from high R&D spending ($200–300M annually) to focused AXON development cycles. Sales & marketing as % of revenue has declined from 35–40% to 25–30% as AXON adoption improves viral adoption (better performance = word-of-mouth). This is fundamental to the margin expansion story.

## Competitive Moat

AppLovin's moat is two-fold: (1) **AXON AI Engine Advantage**—reinforcement-learning models that outperform rule-based competitors (Google, Meta, Mintegral) on ROAS require large volumes of training data and sophisticated ML research. AppLovin's 20+ year legacy in mobile advertising provides proprietary behavioral datasets that competitors struggle to replicate. AXON's learning velocity compounds with network scale: more advertisers using AXON = more training data = better model performance = higher advertiser switching costs. (2) **AppDiscovery Vertical Integration**—owning 100M+ mobile gaming users provides zero-cost-of-capital user-acquisition inventory, lowering AXON's effective cost of inventory acquisition vs. pure-play ad networks (Google, Meta) dependent on external supply sources. This is especially valuable in environments where traditional ad networks face supply constraints.

Competitive threats are material: Google Ads (performance marketing), Meta Ads (social), and Mintegral (Asia-focused) operate larger native networks but lack AppLovin's specialized gaming/mobile vertical focus. Amazon Ads and TikTok Ads are emerging competitors with massive scale but nascent positioning in gaming monetization. Databricks/CDP platforms (Segment, mParticle) threaten to disintermediate attribution analytics (Adjust's core). AppLovin's defensibility hinges on AXON's sustained technical advantage and AppDiscovery's user-acquisition leverage—both are vulnerable to competitive R&D and scale investments from larger platforms.

## Key Investment Considerations

**Bull Case:** AXON 2.0 adoption accelerates across advertiser base (currently 30–40% penetration, target 60–70% by 2027), driving 50–100 bps margin expansion annually. Platform revenue scales to $2.5–3.0B by 2028 at 25–30% growth, with 40–50% EBITDA margins (vs. 30–35% current). AppDiscovery gaming portfolio scaling (new titles, geographic expansion) provides incremental user-acquisition leverage, compounding AXON value. International expansion (APAC, LATAM) provides revenue TAM expansion from $20B TAM (current) to $50B+ (addressable). Rule of 40 trajectory: platform growth (30%+) + margins (40%+) = 70+. Free cash flow generation of $500M–800M annually (based on 2025E EBITDA of $600–700M) supports dividend initiation and share buybacks, potentially re-rating valuation multiples from 15–20x EV/EBITDA toward 20–25x (SaaS comp group benchmarks).

**Bear Case / Primary Risks:** Macro recession impacts advertiser spend on performance marketing; gaming vertical (60% of AppLovin revenue) contracts if consumer discretionary spending deteriorates. AXON competitive parity: Meta or Google matches ROAS improvements through competing AI models; advertiser willingness-to-pay for AXON diminishes. Adjust margin compression: clients self-attribute (in-house post-attribution tools) or migrate to cheaper CDP alternatives. AppDiscovery user churn if game titles underperform (live-ops execution risk) or competitive mobile games capture users. Regulatory headwinds: Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) limits third-party data availability for ML model training; privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA) constrain data monetization. Valuation multiple compression if growth decelerates below 25% or EBITDA margins fail to sustain 35%+.

**Due Diligence Focus Areas:**
- Platform revenue growth rate and year-on-year acceleration/deceleration (indicator of AXON adoption velocity and macro health)
- Adjusted EBITDA margin expansion trajectory (target: 40%+ by 2027; key driver is AXON operational leverage)
- AXON penetration rate (% of advertisers using AXON) and ROAS improvement metrics (% of advertiser base achieving >2% ROAS delta vs. baseline)
- AppDiscovery gaming portfolio performance (MAU trends, user retention, ARPU) and new title pipeline
- Advertiser churn and NRR (net revenue retention; target >120% indicates strong land-and-expand and low churn)

## Valuation Archetype: High-Growth Advertising Platform with Margin Inflection

**Primary Metrics to Track:**
- Platform revenue growth YoY (target: 25–35% annually through 2027; <20% signals competitive pressure or macro softness)
- Adjusted EBITDA margin (% of platform revenue; target: 35–45% by 2027, currently 30–35%)
- Rule of 40 score = revenue growth % + EBITDA margin % (target: 60+ by 2027; currently 55–60)
- Free Cash Flow / Adjusted EBITDA conversion (target: 70–80% reflects capex discipline and working capital efficiency)
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR) by advertiser cohort (target: >120%; indicates AXON value stickiness and upsell success)
